# Privacy text

Two pieces of text cover QHTA Competitions:

1. **Each round's privacy notice.** This is shown above the declarations on
   the form and edited in the round's *Privacy notice* box. Both presets ship
   with one; check it for each new round.
2. **The site privacy policy.** The suggested text below is also registered
   with WordPress, so it appears under **Settings → Privacy → Policy Guide →
   QHTA Competitions** ready to copy into the policy page.

Both describe what the plugin actually does: retention purges personal details
and files rather than deleting entries, and judges see entries without names.
If a round changes any of that (for example a different retention period, or
judging that isn't anonymous), update that round's notice to match.

## Suggested privacy-policy section

### Competitions, awards and bursaries

When you enter a QHTA competition or apply for a bursary or award online, we
collect the details on the form: for example your name, contact details,
school, teaching registration number and membership, and your written piece
or uploaded entry. For the Historical Writing Competition, entries are
submitted by a teacher; we collect the student's name, year level and school
only, and no student contact details. All correspondence goes to the teacher.

Declarations are signed by typing your name. We record that name with the
date, time, IP address and browser, as the record of your signature. If a
referee is needed, we email them a single-use link and record their
confirmation, name and any comment in the same way.

Your details are seen by QHTA administrators. Judges and selection committees
see entries without names or schools wherever possible; an uploaded document
may still show whatever it contains. Your referee sees a summary of your
application without your contact details. Uploaded files are stored privately
and are never at a public web address.

If an entry is awarded or commended, and publication consent was given, QHTA
may publish it with the entrant's name and school in its journal and/or on its
website, and keeps the entry as part of the program's history. For all other
entries, personal details and uploaded files are removed after the retention
period set for each round (12 months after the round closes, unless stated
otherwise on the form).

You can ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, or for it to be
erased, by emailing qhta@qhta.com.au. Awarded or commended entries that have
been published are kept as part of the publication record.

## Handling a request

- **Copy of data:** Tools → Export Personal Data → add the person's email.
  Competition entries, and applications they confirmed as a referee, are
  included.
- **Erasure:** Tools → Erase Personal Data. Entries that weren't awarded or
  commended are purged the same way retention purges them. Awarded/commended
  entries are kept and the tool says so; remove those by hand only if
  publication hasn't happened or has been withdrawn.
